Recover text from a scanned PDF

English OCR runs in your browser. The engine and language files download from a version-pinned CDN; this page does not upload your PDF or page images. Keep the original PDF. OCR can misread numbers, tables and mandatory clauses.

Limits: PDF only, 10 MiB, up to 5 pages, bounded image resolution. Split larger scans. This is text recovery, not certified document conversion or exact layout preservation.

Downloaded text includes original-file hash and physical page markers. Upload it as a clearly labelled derived source; citations refer to that derived text. Retain the original PDF and verify requirements against it.
