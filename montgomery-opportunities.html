What this feed is

The feed lists active solicitations from Montgomery County, Maryland, published on the county open-data portal. The dataset is updated daily and covers solicitations issued in the past 7 years. The feed publishes no per-solicitation link, so each result links to the county solicitation search page. When a notice states no close date the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing. No estimated value is published by this feed. The feed refreshes about once an hour. Attachments are not imported.

Questions about this feed

Is searching Montgomery County notices free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the county solicitation search takes you to the Montgomery County procurement site.

What is this feed?

It lists active solicitations from Montgomery County, Maryland, published on the county open-data portal. The dataset is updated daily and covers solicitations issued in the past 7 years.

Why does every result link to the county search page?

The feed publishes no per-solicitation link, so each result links to the county solicitation search page. Search the solicitation number there to open the original notice.

Does a listed close date mean the bid is still open?

No. A listed date does not confirm that a bid remains open. Verify the closing time, amendments, eligibility, and documents on the original notice and the agency's submission portal before you act.

Can I shred a Montgomery County notice?

Yes. Send the notice details to the free shredder, then paste or upload the full solicitation for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Attachments are not imported.
