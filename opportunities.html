How to use this feed

Start with a word from the title, a NAICS code, a PSC code, or a set-aside, and the feed searches live federal solicitations on SAM.gov, the official US government site for federal contract opportunities. Results show the newest notices first, or the nearest response deadline first if you change the sort. Open any result to read the original notice on SAM.gov, and use the Shred this link to send an open solicitation to the free shredder.

  1. Search by title keyword, six-digit NAICS code, four-character PSC code, or set-aside.
  2. Open the original notice on SAM.gov and check the deadline, eligibility, attachments, and amendments.
  3. Send an open solicitation to the free shredder for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review.
  4. Run the bid checker to judge whether the opportunity is worth pursuing before you invest in a response.

What the filters mean

Each filter matches a field the agency puts on the notice.

  • Title keyword: a word or phrase from the solicitation title, up to 100 characters.
  • NAICS: the six-digit North American Industry Classification System code for the work. Agencies list it on every notice.
  • PSC: the four-character Product Service Code for what the government is buying.
  • Set-aside: limits the results to solicitations reserved for a category of small business: small business, partial small business, 8(a), 8(a) sole source, HUBZone, service-disabled veteran-owned, women-owned, and economically disadvantaged women-owned.

What happens after you find a solicitation

Finding the notice is the start, not the finish. Every result links to the free shredder, which turns an open solicitation into a numbered requirement list and a preview of the items that need human review, so you can see the compliance work before you commit to bidding. The bid checker then scores the opportunity against five fit factors, and the workspace turns the requirement list into a draft built from your own evidence. Only open solicitations can be shredded. Award notices and other notice types that are not open solicitations cannot be shredded, and the shredder says so instead of guessing.

Questions about this feed

Is searching federal opportunities free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the original notice takes you to SAM.gov.

How current are the results?

Results come live from SAM.gov and are cached for up to one hour. Always verify the deadline, eligibility, attachments and amendments on the original notice before you decide to bid.

What is a NAICS code?

A six-digit code that classifies the industry a solicitation belongs to. Federal agencies list the NAICS code on each notice. If you do not know yours, start with a title keyword search and read the NAICS codes on the notices that match.

What does a set-aside mean?

A set-aside limits competition to a category of small business, for example small business, 8(a), HUBZone, service-disabled veteran-owned, women-owned, or economically disadvantaged women-owned firms. Use the set-aside filter to see only the solicitations reserved for a category your business qualifies for.

Can I shred a solicitation I find here?

Yes. Every result has a Shred this link that sends the open solicitation to the free shredder, which returns a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Award notices and other non-solicitation notices cannot be shredded.
