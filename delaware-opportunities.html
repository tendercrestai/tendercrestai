What the Delaware feed is

The feed lists open bids from the State of Delaware, published by the Office of Management and Budget, Government Support Services, on the state open-data portal. The dataset is updated weekly, so a new bid may appear here up to a week after it opens. The feed publishes a Delaware agency code rather than an agency name, so the card shows the code as published. When a bid states no deadline the feed can read, the card says the deadline is not stated instead of guessing. No estimated value is published by this feed. The feed refreshes about once an hour. Attachments are not imported.

Questions about this feed

Is searching Delaware bids free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the original bid takes you to the Delaware bid portal.

What is the Delaware open bids feed?

It lists open bids from the State of Delaware, published by the Office of Management and Budget, Government Support Services, on the state open-data portal. The dataset is updated weekly.

Why is the agency shown as a code?

The feed publishes a Delaware agency code rather than an agency name, so the card shows the code as published. Check the original bid for the agency name.

Does a listed deadline mean the bid is still open?

No. A listed date does not confirm that a bid remains open. Verify the closing time, amendments, eligibility, and documents on the original bid before you act.

Can I shred a Delaware bid?

Yes. Send the bid details to the free shredder, then paste or upload the full solicitation for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Attachments are not imported.
