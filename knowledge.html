        Team access

        Only the organization owner can invite people, change their role, or deactivate access. The owner's access cannot be changed here.

        What can each role do?
        • Owner: upload, search, edit, approve, inspect activity and manage access.
        • Editor: upload, search and edit answers.
        • Reviewer: search, edit and approve answers, and inspect activity.
        • Viewer: read documents and answers and search evidence.

        All active members can read drafts. Roles do not create private reviewer-only drafts.
