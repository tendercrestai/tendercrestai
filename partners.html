All partner tools

  • TenderCrest AI — TenderCrest AI itself is the first product on this page: evidence-backed RFP responses, reviewed together.
  • Deltek GovWin IQ — Market intelligence for government contracting: find opportunities, track agencies, and follow competitors before a solicitation drops. Who it is for: Teams that need a pipeline before the RFP arrives. How it fits: Find the opportunity in GovWin, then bring the RFP into TenderCrest to build the response.
  • SAM.gov — The US government's free system for federal contract opportunities, plus entity registration. Who it is for: Any business bidding on federal work. How it fits: Search SAM.gov, then shred the solicitation in TenderCrest's free shredder.
  • HubSpot CRM — A CRM for tracking capture, contacts, and follow-ups across a pipeline of bids. Who it is for: Teams managing many pursuits at once. How it fits: Track the pursuit in HubSpot; build the response in TenderCrest.
  • DocuSign — Electronic signatures for proposals, NDAs, and teaming agreements. Who it is for: Anyone who needs a signed document back fast. How it fits: Get the paperwork signed in DocuSign; keep the proposal itself in TenderCrest.
  • APMP — The Association of Proposal Management Professionals: training, certification, and the Body of Knowledge for proposal work. Who it is for: Proposal professionals building their craft. How it fits: Learn the practice with APMP; apply it in TenderCrest.
  • Lohfeld Consulting — Capture and proposal training and consulting, including the Lohfeld Insights blog. Who it is for: Teams that want coaching on capture and proposals. How it fits: Train with Lohfeld; run the work in TenderCrest.
  • Proposal services partners — Bid-writing, capture, and proposal consultancies that use TenderCrest AI with their clients. Who it is for: Small businesses that want a consultant to run the bid. How it fits: Work with a partner firm, or become one: partners earn 20% on paid customers they refer.
