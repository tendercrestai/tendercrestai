About TenderCrest AI

Our mission is to make sure good work wins bids. We help proposal teams answer every RFP from evidence they already trust, with every requirement checked and every claim reviewed by a human before it goes out. The same mission covers grant proposals: small nonprofits and grant writers face the same paperwork problem with funding opportunities, and Grant Mode brings the same evidence-backed approach to NOFOs.

Founded in October 2026, headquartered in Seattle, Washington.

The problem

The problem we keep hearing is straightforward: proposal teams rewrite the same evidence for every bid, then find the compliance gaps after submission. Good teams lose work they should have won, not on quality, but on paperwork. We build for the teams doing the work, and everything we add starts from what those teams tell us they struggle with.

What TenderCrest AI does

TenderCrest AI helps small and midsize businesses turn complex RFPs into structured, evidence-backed proposal drafts. It is built for consulting firms, IT services providers, and proposal teams, and it brings bid assessment, company knowledge, drafting, and compliance review into one workspace. Grant Mode applies the same workflow to grant proposals for nonprofits, small contractors, and grant writers: it shreds a funding opportunity (NOFO) into a requirements checklist, checks every claim against approved evidence, drafts in English or Spanish, and exports a compliance matrix.

With TenderCrest AI, teams can:

  • Assess bid fit and spot eligibility gaps and risks.
  • Shred a funding opportunity (NOFO), check fit, and draft a grant proposal with the same evidence-backed review.
  • Reuse approved company information, case studies, and supporting evidence.
  • Generate and edit proposal drafts aligned with RFP requirements.
  • Review requirement coverage, unsupported claims, and potential inconsistencies.
  • Collaborate on reviews and approvals.
  • Export proposals to Word or PDF.

Our approach

Our approach is simple: AI assists, people review and approve. It runs on our own AI pipeline, built specifically for proposal work, with leading language models doing the extraction and people doing the review. Source traceability and visible gaps help teams make informed decisions, without treating AI-generated content as verified fact.
