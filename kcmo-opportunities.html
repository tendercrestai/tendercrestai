What Bonfire is

Bonfire is the procurement portal Kansas City uses for city solicitations. This feed lists its open public opportunities from the Kansas City Bonfire hub, with the close date read from each notice. When a notice does not state a close date in a form the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing. No estimated value is published by this feed. The feed refreshes about once an hour. Attachments are not imported.

Questions about this feed

Is searching Kansas City notices free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the original notice takes you to the Kansas City Bonfire portal.

What is Bonfire?

Bonfire is the procurement portal Kansas City uses for city solicitations. This feed lists its open public opportunities, with the close date read from each notice.

Does a listed close date mean the bid is still open?

No. A listed date does not confirm that a bid remains open. Verify the closing time, amendments, eligibility, and documents on the original notice and the agency's submission portal before you act.

Why do some notices say the close date is not stated?

The Bonfire feed states the close date inside the notice text, so this feed reads it from there. When a notice does not state a close date in a form the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing.

Can I shred a Kansas City notice?

Yes. Send the available notice text to the free shredder for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Attachments are not imported, so paste or upload the full solicitation for a complete review.
