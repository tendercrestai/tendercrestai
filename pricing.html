Try your selected plan free for 14 days

A payment card is required at Stripe checkout. Use the same email address you use to sign in to TenderCrest AI. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your selected plan automatically starts billing at the monthly price shown above. Manage cancellation through the Billing Portal.

Your trial includes up to 5 proposals on Pro, 20 on Growth, or 60 on Enterprise across the entire 14-day period. It does not reset during the trial. Monthly allowances start with the paid billing period after a successful payment.

The standalone free Enterprise offer is no longer available. Existing legacy free trials keep their original expiry and do not automatically become paid subscriptions.

What uses a proposal credit?

A submission to the existing emailed AI-draft service uses its plan or trial allowance. The paid plans retain their 5, 20 and 60 monthly proposal allowances. Editing a workspace section or exporting a saved workspace package does not deduct those credits.

Questions about pricing

What does the 14-day trial include?

Your trial includes up to 5 proposals on Pro, 20 on Growth, or 60 on Enterprise across the entire 14-day period. It does not reset during the trial. A payment card is required at Stripe checkout. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your selected plan automatically starts billing at the monthly price.

Are plans billed per seat?

No. Plans are billed by proposal volume, not per seat. Pro, Growth and Enterprise include monthly allowances of 5, 20 and 60 submissions to the existing emailed AI-draft service. Workspace section editing and saved-package exports do not deduct those credits.

How does the 30-day Product Hunt trial extension work?

The Product Hunt launch offers a 30-day trial instead of the standard 14 days. Claim it by emailing hello@tendercrestai.com.
