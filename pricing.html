What grant support actually costs

Hiring a grant writer commonly costs $5,000–$15,000 per proposal and takes 4–8 weeks (Granted AI pricing page, November 2026). Grant-writing tools commonly charge $29–$150 a month (Granted AI $29–$89, Grantable $50–$150, November 2026), and discovery-only tools start at $299 a month. TenderCrest Grant Mode starts at $19 a month for one complete grant proposal, with NOFO shredding, evidence checking, English and Spanish drafting, and the compliance matrix included. No award is guaranteed.

What uses a proposal credit?

A submission to the existing emailed AI-draft service uses its plan or trial allowance. The paid plans retain their 5, 20 and 60 monthly proposal allowances. Editing a workspace section or exporting a saved workspace package does not deduct those credits.

One grant proposal is one submission to the emailed AI-draft service for one funding opportunity. Grant Starter, Grant Plus and Grant Pro include 1, 5 and 15 grant proposals a month. Workspace editing and saved-package exports do not use grant credits.

Questions about pricing

What does the 14-day trial include?

Your trial includes up to 5 proposals on Pro, 20 on Growth, or 60 on Enterprise across the entire 14-day period. Grant trials include 1 grant proposal on Grant Starter, 5 on Grant Plus, or 15 on Grant Pro. It does not reset during the trial. A payment card is required at Stripe checkout. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your selected plan automatically starts billing at the monthly price.

Are plans billed per seat?

No. Plans are billed by proposal volume, not per seat. Pro, Growth and Enterprise include monthly allowances of 5, 20 and 60 submissions to the existing emailed AI-draft service. Grant Starter, Grant Plus and Grant Pro include monthly allowances of 1, 5 and 15 grant proposals. Workspace section editing and saved-package exports do not deduct those credits.

What counts as a grant proposal?

One grant proposal is one submission to the emailed AI-draft service for one funding opportunity (NOFO). Workspace editing and saved-package exports do not use grant credits.

Can I hold a grant plan and an RFP plan at the same time?

Not in this release. A customer holds either a grant plan or an RFP plan. Grant plans are standalone: Grant Starter $19, Grant Plus $49, Grant Pro $99 per month.

Do you train AI on my documents?

No. Your customer data is not used to train models. Your evidence and drafts stay in your account and are not used to train a shared model. See the Terms and Security pages for data handling.

Can I get an invoice or pay by purchase order?

Stripe receipts and invoices are available through the Billing Portal. If your organization needs a purchase order, contact us before checkout and we will confirm what is possible.

What happens to my drafts if I cancel?

Canceling stops new grant submissions when the billing period ends. Your saved projects are not deleted by canceling; delete a project explicitly from the workspace if you need it gone. Manage cancellation through the Billing Portal.
