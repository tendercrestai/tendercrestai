Why teams choose TenderCrest

  • Transparent monthly prices available before a sales conversation.
  • Compliance gap flags and matrix exports for reviewing government and commercial requirements.
  • A fictional interactive demo you can inspect without signup or confidential uploads.
  • Published emailed-draft allowances of 5, 20 or 60 proposals. Workspace section editing and saved-package exports do not consume those credits; workspace access remains subject to its own terms.

When Responsive is the better fit

  • You run concurrent RFPs and questionnaires across multiple business units and need the governance and access controls described in its higher editions.
  • You prefer an annual platform arrangement, unlimited response projects and a configurable license/add-on package. Unlimited projects does not mean unlimited user licenses.
  • If that describes your organization, Responsive may be the better fit. Smaller teams can evaluate TenderCrest’s requirement matching, evidence reuse, compliance flags and review workflow against their own needs. This is not a claim of feature parity.

Frequently asked questions

Is TenderCrest cheaper than Responsive?

TenderCrest Pro is $299/month, or $3,588 over 12 months before taxes. Responsive lists Emerging Edition from $10,000 and describes its platform subscription as annual, with user licenses and add-ons as separate components. The products and included scope differ; compare the full quote rather than treating the plans as equivalent.

Can I try TenderCrest without talking to sales?

Yes. The interactive demo uses fictional samples and needs no signup or card. Paid-plan trials last 14 days, require a payment card, and bill the selected plan automatically afterward unless canceled.

Does TenderCrest work for federal bids?

Yes. TenderCrest supports approved company evidence, requirement matching, compliance gap flags, and DOCX, PDF and compliance matrix exports. Human reviewers must verify the solicitation and submission rules; the tool does not certify compliance or guarantee an award.

What happens after the trial?

Your selected Pro, Growth or Enterprise plan automatically starts billing after the 14-day trial unless you cancel. A payment card is required. The existing emailed-draft service includes 5, 20 or 60 proposals across the entire trial, then the corresponding monthly allowance after successful payment.
