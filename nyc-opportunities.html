What City Record Online is

City Record Online is the City of New York's official journal of procurement notices, published as open data through NYC Open Data. This feed lists its procurement solicitation notices with a listed deadline of today or later, in New York time. The feed refreshes about once an hour. During a source outage, the last successful snapshot may be shown for up to 24 hours with a warning. Attachments are not imported.

Questions about this feed

Is searching NYC notices free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the original notice takes you to City Record Online.

What is City Record Online?

City Record Online is the City of New York's official journal of procurement notices, published as open data. This feed lists its procurement solicitation notices with a listed deadline of today or later, in New York time.

Does a listed deadline mean the bid is still open?

No. A listed date does not confirm that a bid remains open. Verify the closing time, amendments, eligibility, and documents on the original notice and the agency's submission portal before you act.

How fresh is this feed?

The feed refreshes about once an hour. During a source outage, the last successful snapshot may be shown for up to 24 hours with a warning.

Can I shred a NYC notice?

Yes. Send the available notice text to the free shredder for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Attachments are not imported, so paste or upload the full solicitation for a complete review.
