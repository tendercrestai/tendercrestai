  • You need a general writing assistant for drafting, brainstorming, research or everyday documents, not a proposal workflow.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT or Claude write an RFP response?

They can draft one from material you supply. They cannot prove every requirement was answered, enforce that answers come only from approved evidence, or record a human approval. Those are workflow features, not writing features.

Is TenderCrest cheaper than ChatGPT or Claude?

It depends on the plan. ChatGPT Plus is $20/month, and ChatGPT Business and Claude Team start at $20/user/month. TenderCrest Pro is $299/month for 5 proposals, billed monthly. You are paying for the evidence, coverage and review workflow, not for drafting alone.

Does TenderCrest train on my data?

No. Your customer data is not used to train models. AI extraction runs through OpenAI's API and Airtable AI under their terms, which do not use customer inputs for training, and TenderCrest has no model of its own.

Can I try TenderCrest without talking to sales?

Yes. The interactive demo uses fictional samples and needs no signup or card. Paid-plan trials last 14 days, require a payment card, and bill the selected plan automatically afterward unless canceled.
