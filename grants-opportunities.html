What Grants.gov is

Grants.gov is the US government's single portal for federal grant opportunities. This feed lists its posted and forecasted grants, with forecasted grants announced but not yet open. The search feed lists the grant number, title, agency and dates, but not award amounts or eligibility, so open the grant on Grants.gov for the award ceiling, award floor, eligibility and the full funding opportunity. The feed refreshes about once an hour. Attachments are not imported.

Questions about this feed

Is searching federal grants free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the original grant takes you to Grants.gov.

Are these grants or contracts?

Grants. This feed lists federal grant opportunities from Grants.gov, not federal contracts. Contracts live on the SAM.gov feed.

Why don't results show award amounts?

The Grants.gov search feed lists the grant number, title, agency and dates, but not award amounts or eligibility. Open the grant on Grants.gov for the award ceiling, award floor, eligibility and the full funding opportunity.

How fresh is this feed?

Results come live from Grants.gov and are cached for up to one hour. Always verify the deadline, eligibility, attachments and amendments on the original grant before you apply.

Can I shred a grant?

Yes. Send the available grant synopsis to the free shredder for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Attachments are not imported, so paste or upload the full funding opportunity for a complete review.
