Frequently asked questions

Is TenderCrest cheaper than GrantParty?

Yes. TenderCrest Grant Mode is $19, $49 or $99 a month for 1, 5 or 15 complete grant proposals. GrantParty is $249 a month or $2,490 a year for 1 organization, plus $99 a month per extra organization. GrantParty also offers a $100 pilot: 1 complete application plus 30 days, with no subscription.

Can I try TenderCrest without talking to sales?

Yes. The interactive demo uses fictional samples and needs no signup or card. Grant-plan trials last 14 days, require a payment card, and bill the selected plan automatically afterward unless canceled.

Does TenderCrest draft the budget?

TenderCrest includes a simple budget table and budget narrative on every grant plan. That is not a full budget builder: no indirect-cost rates, modular budgets, multi-year schedules or sub-awards. GrantParty drafts the budget as part of the full application.

Does TenderCrest train on my data?

No. Your customer data is not used to train models. Your evidence and drafts stay in your account and are not used to train a shared model.
