Why teams choose TenderCrest

  • Transparent monthly prices available before a sales conversation.
  • Compliance gap flags and matrix exports for reviewing government and commercial requirements.
  • A fictional interactive demo you can inspect without signup or confidential uploads.
  • Published emailed-draft allowances of 5, 20 or 60 proposals. Workspace section editing and saved-package exports do not consume those credits; workspace access remains subject to its own terms.

When Loopio is the better fit

  • You answer a high volume of repeatable RFPs, RFIs and security questionnaires, and a large library of reusable answers is central to your process.
  • You want a quoted package starting with 10 seats, or higher-tier multi-step reviews and separate business units. Confirm the relevant plan and terms with Loopio.
  • If that describes your organization, Loopio may be the better fit. If your team is smaller and needs requirement matching, evidence reuse, compliance flags and review, evaluate TenderCrest alongside it. No lower-price claim is made without a comparable Loopio quote.

Frequently asked questions

Is TenderCrest cheaper than Loopio?

TenderCrest starts at $299/month billed monthly. Loopio’s current official pricing page does not publish a starting dollar amount; Foundations includes 10 seats. A current Loopio quote is needed for a defensible price comparison.

Can I try TenderCrest without talking to sales?

Yes. The interactive demo uses fictional samples and needs no signup or card. Paid-plan trials last 14 days, require a payment card, and bill the selected plan automatically afterward unless canceled.

Does TenderCrest work for federal bids?

Yes. TenderCrest supports approved company evidence, requirement matching, compliance gap flags, and DOCX, PDF and compliance matrix exports. Human reviewers must verify the solicitation and submission rules; the tool does not certify compliance or guarantee an award.

Do I pay per seat on TenderCrest?

The published Pro, Growth and Enterprise prices set monthly allowances of 5, 20 and 60 submissions to the existing emailed AI-draft service. Workspace section editing and saved-package exports do not deduct those credits. This does not promise unlimited workspace seats or access; review current workspace terms on the pricing page.
