What PASS is

PASS is the Procurement Automated Support System, the District of Columbia's procurement system. This feed lists its open solicitations from the District's open-data portal. The feed uses the close date as the operative deadline, because the due-date field is not consistently published. Solicitations are advertised through OCP eSourcing and bids need a registered eSourcing account. When a solicitation states no close date the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing. No estimated value is published by this feed. The feed refreshes about once an hour. Attachments are not imported.

Questions about this feed

Is searching DC solicitations free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the DC procurement portal takes you to the District's Office of Contracting and Procurement site.

What is PASS?

PASS is the Procurement Automated Support System, the District of Columbia's procurement system. This feed lists its open solicitations from the District's open-data portal.

Why does every result link to the OCP site?

The feed publishes no per-solicitation link, so each result links to the DC Office of Contracting and Procurement site. Search the solicitation number there to open the original notice.

Does a listed close date mean the bid is still open?

No. A listed date does not confirm that a bid remains open. Verify the closing time, amendments, eligibility, and documents on the original notice and the agency's submission portal before you act.

Can I shred a DC solicitation?

Yes. Send the solicitation details to the free shredder, then paste or upload the full solicitation for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Attachments are not imported.
