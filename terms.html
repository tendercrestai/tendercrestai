The service

TenderCrest AI helps proposal teams write evidence-backed RFP and proposal responses. You can paste a solicitation or a SAM.gov link to get a numbered requirement list, match approved company evidence to requirements, see compliance gap flags, collaborate on a saved proposal package with team review, and export DOCX, PDF, and compliance-matrix files.

The free tools (RFP shredder, bid checker, and the SAM.gov opportunity feed) are available without an account, subject to daily limits. The interactive demo uses fictional samples and needs no signup or card.
