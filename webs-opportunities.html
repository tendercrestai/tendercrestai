What WEBS is

WEBS is Washington's Electronic Business Solution, the State of Washington's procurement portal. This feed lists the public bid calendar's soonest-closing solicitation notices, with the close date read from each listing. When a listing does not state a close date in a form the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing. No estimated value is published by this feed. The feed refreshes about once an hour. Attachments are not imported. Full solicitation documents need a free WEBS login.

Questions about this feed

Is searching Washington notices free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the original notice takes you to the WEBS bid calendar.

What is WEBS?

WEBS is Washington's Electronic Business Solution, the State of Washington's procurement portal. This feed lists the public bid calendar's soonest-closing solicitation notices.

Does a listed close date mean the bid is still open?

No. A listed date does not confirm that a bid remains open. Verify the closing time, amendments, eligibility, and documents on the original notice and the agency's submission portal before you act.

Why do some notices say the close date is not stated?

The WEBS calendar states the close date on each listing, so this feed reads it from there. When a listing does not state a close date in a form the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing.

Can I shred a Washington notice?

Yes. Send the available calendar text to the free shredder for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Full solicitation documents need a free WEBS login, so paste or upload the full solicitation for a complete review.
