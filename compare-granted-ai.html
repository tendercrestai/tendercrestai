Why teams choose TenderCrest

When Granted AI is the better fit

  • You want a free plan with 1 draft a month and no card, rather than a 14-day trial.
  • You want coached Q and A and a 6-reviewer simulated board before you submit.
  • Your work is mostly NIH, NSF or SBIR, and you value the funded or refunded guarantee. Read its fine print and submission minimums first.

Frequently asked questions

Is TenderCrest cheaper than Granted AI?

At entry, yes: TenderCrest Grant Mode starts at $19/month for 1 complete grant proposal, and Granted AI Basic is $29/month for 3 drafts. At the top, Granted AI Professional is $89/month ($57/month annual) for unlimited drafts, which is less than TenderCrest Grant Pro at $99/month for 15 grants, but Professional caps full seats at 3, with the rest comment-only. Granted AI also has a free plan with 1 draft a month; TenderCrest has no free plan, and its free slice is the Grants.gov feed and the free NOFO shredder, with no signup or card.

Can I try TenderCrest without talking to sales?

Yes. The interactive demo uses fictional samples and needs no signup or card. Grant-plan trials last 14 days, require a payment card, and bill the selected plan automatically afterward unless canceled.

Does TenderCrest submit my grant to Grants.gov?

No. TenderCrest does not submit to Grants.gov, and Granted AI does not either. TenderCrest gives submission guidance and a Grants.gov tracking note, and exports the proposal and compliance matrix for you to submit through the channel named in the NOFO.

Does TenderCrest train on my data?

No. Your customer data is not used to train models. Your evidence and drafts stay in your account and are not used to train a shared model.
