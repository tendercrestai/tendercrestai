What RAMP is

RAMP is the procurement portal the City of Los Angeles uses for solicitations, covering the city, LA County and LAUSD. This feed lists its open public opportunities from the City of Los Angeles open-data portal, published under a CC0 public domain dedication. When a notice states no close date the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing. No estimated value is published by this feed. The feed refreshes about once an hour. Attachments are not imported.

Questions about this feed

Is searching Los Angeles notices free?

Yes. Searching the feed needs no signup, email or card. Opening the original notice takes you to the RAMP portal.

What is RAMP?

RAMP is the procurement portal the City of Los Angeles uses for solicitations, covering the city, LA County and LAUSD. This feed lists its open public opportunities from the City of Los Angeles open-data portal, published under a CC0 public domain dedication.

Does a listed close date mean the bid is still open?

No. A listed date does not confirm that a bid remains open. Verify the closing time, amendments, eligibility, and documents on the original notice and the agency's submission portal before you act.

Why do some notices say the close date is not stated?

The RAMP feed publishes a close date for most notices. When a notice states no close date the feed can read, the card says the close date is not stated instead of guessing.

Can I shred a Los Angeles notice?

Yes. Send the notice details to the free shredder, then paste or upload the full solicitation for a numbered requirement list and a preview of items needing review. Attachments are not imported.
