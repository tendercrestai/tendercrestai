Why teams choose TenderCrest

  • Transparent monthly prices available before a sales conversation: $19, $49 or $99, keyed to grants per month.
  • NOFO shredding as the headline, with an exportable compliance matrix that proves nothing was missed.
  • Evidence check with citations. Gaps are flagged for a person to fill in, never invented.
  • English and Spanish drafting from the same evidence.
  • A free Grants.gov feed and free NOFO shredder, with no signup or card.

When Grantable is the better fit

  • You want a content library and org profile as the core of your workflow, with prospecting and funder research built in.
  • You want a free plan with daily messages rather than a 14-day trial.
  • You run a multi-client agency and want the Agency Hub, at $300 a month on top of a paid plan.
  • If that describes your organization, Grantable may be the better fit. If your team is smaller and needs NOFO shredding, evidence checking, a compliance matrix and Spanish drafting, evaluate TenderCrest alongside it.

Frequently asked questions

Is TenderCrest cheaper than Grantable?

At the entry and top of the paid range, yes. TenderCrest Grant Mode starts at $19/month for 1 grant and tops out at $99/month for 15 grants. Grantable starts at $50/month (Starter) and Pro is $150/month. Grantable also has a free $0 plan with 5 messages a day; TenderCrest has no free plan, and its free slice is the Grants.gov feed and the free NOFO shredder, with no signup or card.

Can I try TenderCrest without talking to sales?

Yes. The interactive demo uses fictional samples and needs no signup or card. Grant-plan trials last 14 days, require a payment card, and bill the selected plan automatically afterward unless canceled.

Does TenderCrest submit my grant to Grants.gov?

No. TenderCrest does not submit to Grants.gov, and Grantable does not either. TenderCrest gives submission guidance and a Grants.gov tracking note, and exports the proposal and compliance matrix for you to submit through the channel named in the NOFO.

Does TenderCrest train on my data?

No. Your customer data is not used to train models. Your evidence and drafts stay in your account and are not used to train a shared model.
