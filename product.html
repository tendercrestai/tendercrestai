The walkthrough below uses fictional Harbor Borough and Cedar Support. It illustrates review decisions; it is not a customer case study or a promise of compliance or contract awards.

Walk through a fictional IT support response

Open each step to see the evidence, decision, and next action. This walkthrough makes no AI requests and submits no proposal.

1. Create your private organization library

Sign in with a verified account, create an organization, and upload company documents you are authorized to process. Owners can invite editors, reviewers, or viewers using a link bound to the invited verified email.

In this example, Cedar Support adds its operating note. Uploading and indexing requires consent to send extracted source excerpts to the embedding provider.

Understand the Knowledge Base data flow
2. Find a quotation you can verify

Search filenames, source text, draft or approved answers, and permitted activity. Choose AI search for meaning-based retrieval, or use text search without an embedding request for that query.

Example source quotation · operating note §A
The service desk acknowledges priority-one incidents within 30 minutes.

Open the original source, check the context, and select the quotation as a citation. A matching sentence supports a review; it does not independently prove delivery capability.

3. Save a cited answer and approve its revision

A person writes the answer using the selected quotation: “Cedar Support's operating note states a 30-minute acknowledgement target for priority-one incidents.” An owner or reviewer can approve the exact saved revision. An edit clears that approval.

All active organization members can read sources and draft answers. Reviewer approval is a human decision, not an external certification.

4. Review the RFP matrix and its evidence gaps

The compliance workspace extracts RFP text for review before AI analysis. Inspect each requirement and its source, then supply company evidence and a reference. Its response-owner field is a label; it does not grant team access.

Bring selected, reviewed Knowledge Base evidence into the matrix's evidence fields. Evidence selection between these workspaces is currently a manual step. The matrix supports CSV and XLSX exports.

Open the compliance workspace
5. Prepare a proposal input and review the delivered draft

Use the proposal workspace to submit reviewed requirements and verified company qualifications. The proposal workflow generates a first draft and delivers it by email. Verify its claims, citations, prices, and submission instructions before use.

The sample draft leaves the unsupported certification as an explicit gap. Use it to inspect the review standard, rather than to make an unsupported claim.
