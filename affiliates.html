Become a TenderCrest AI partner

Program terms in brief

  • 20% commission on each referred customer's monthly payment.
  • First 12 payments only, within the customer's first 12 months.
  • 90-day referral cookie. If someone signs up through your link within 90 days, the referral is yours.
  • Paid subscriptions only. No commission on trials or demo signups.
  • Commissions become due 30 days after each payment.
  • Payouts via PayPal, with no minimum payout.

How it works

  1. Sign up free. Create your partner account; there is no application and no fee.
  2. Share your referral link. Send it to businesses that respond to RFPs, or mention TenderCrest AI to your clients.
  3. Earn when they pay. When a referred customer pays, your commission becomes due 30 days after that payment, and is paid out via PayPal.

Who can join

Anyone who can refer customers may join. There is no application and no fee. If you already have a partner account, sign in to the affiliate portal to find your referral link and track referrals.

Two ways to partner

  • Affiliate partners (this page) — refer customers and earn 20% of their first 12 payments. This is the self-serve track described above.
  • Proposal services partners — bid-writing, capture, and proposal consultancies that use TenderCrest AI with their clients. These firms are listed on the Partner tools page. If your firm uses TenderCrest AI with clients and would like to be listed there, contact hello@tendercrestai.com.

What partners get

  • A mutual link exchange with TenderCrest AI, by agreement for referral partners who publish a partner page.
  • A directory spotlight once listed in the partner directory below.
  • A guided demo walkthrough for your team.
  • The interactive demo and sample deliverables as self-serve training: try the interactive demo or browse sample deliverables.

Frequently asked questions

When do I get paid?

Commissions become due 30 days after each payment. Payouts are made via PayPal, with no minimum payout.

What happens if a referred customer cancels or gets a refund?

Commission is earned only on paid invoices. If a referred payment is fully refunded, no commission is due on that payment.

Do I earn commission on free trials?

No. Commission is paid only on paid subscriptions, not on trials or demo signups.

How long does my referral link last?

90 days. If someone signs up through your link within 90 days, the referral is yours.

How many payments earn commission?

The first 12 monthly payments within the customer's first 12 months.

Is there an application or a fee to join?

No. Anyone who can refer customers may join. Signup is free and self-serve.
